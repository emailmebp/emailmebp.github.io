Sparklecorn privacy policy

Last updated October 24, 2026

Sparklecorn is a game for families. It doesn't collect, store or share any personal information, and it never sends anything off your device.

What the game keeps, and where

  • Your beast's name, which you type in, and each player's name in your family.
  • Game progress: levels, stars, gems, the Style shop, the Life Journey and the Palace Party.
  • Settings, and how long each player has played this week, which a grown-up can see behind a question in Settings.

If you add the Sparklecorn widget to your Home Screen, the game leaves it a picture of the current player's beast, its name and its friendship level, in storage on your device that only Sparklecorn and its widget can read.

All of this stays in the app's own storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it is deleted when you delete the app. Removing a player in the game deletes that player's game.

What the game doesn't do

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No advertising, and no analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No internet connection is used, and there are no links out of the app.
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos.

Read-aloud

If you turn on "Read the story aloud", the story is spoken by your device's built-in voice, which works on the device.

Children

Sparklecorn is made for children and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws by not collecting any personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
