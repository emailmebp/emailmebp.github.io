Sparklecorn privacy policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Sparklecorn is a game for families. It doesn't collect, store or share any personal information, and it never sends anything to us or to anyone else.

What the game keeps, and where

  • Your beast's name, which you type in, and each player's name in your family.
  • Game progress: levels, stars, gems, the Style shop, the Life Journey and the Palace Party.
  • Settings, and how long each player has played this week, which a grown-up can see behind a question in Settings.

If you add the Sparklecorn widget to your Home Screen, the game leaves it a picture of the current player's beast, its name, its friendship level and how many lands they've saved, in storage on your device that only Sparklecorn and its widget can read.

All of this is kept in the app's own storage on your device. If iCloud is turned on for your Apple Account, the game also keeps a copy in your own iCloud, using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so each player's game follows your family to your other iPhone or iPad signed in to the same Apple Account. That copy is yours, in your iCloud: we can't see it, and it is never sent to us or to anyone else. Without iCloud, everything stays on your device. It is deleted from your device when you delete the app. Removing a player in the game deletes that player's game on your other devices too, unless one of them is still playing as that player: that device keeps their game and shares it with the family again. A removed player's copy in your family's iCloud is kept for two weeks, then it goes.

What the game doesn't do

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No advertising, and no analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No connection to us or anyone else. The game only uses Apple's own services: iCloud keeping your family's copy when iCloud is on, and, at most once a day as the game opens, Apple's App Store asked whether a newer version of Sparklecorn is out (it sends Apple only the app's App Store ID and your App Store country). Links to our help page, this policy, the App Store review page and the App Store update page sit behind the grown-up question.
  • No location, camera, microphone or contacts. The game never looks at your photos: it only adds a coloring page to Photos when a grown-up taps Save, past the grown-up question.

Read-aloud

If you turn on "Read the story aloud", the story is spoken by your device's built-in voice, which works on the device.

Children

Sparklecorn is made for children and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws by not collecting any personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
