Sparklecorn help

Sparklecorn is a family iPhone game: name your own mystical beast, ride its carriage up the board, and bring the sparkle back to the Mystic Kingdom one land at a time.

Need help, or found a problem? Email emailmebp.bp@gmail.com. Say which iPhone you have and what happened, and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

How do I steer?

On Easy, touch anywhere and slide one finger: your beast follows it. On Hard, pull the two rope handles: drag down on a side to pull that rope, up to let it out. You pick Easy or Hard when a new game starts, and can change it in Settings. If steering feels too quick for small hands, try Settings > Easier to steer.

Can the whole family play?

Yes. Tap the name chip at the top of the start screen and add a player. Each family member gets their own beast, progress and Kingdom Book. On Hard, two players can share one carriage, a handle each.

Where is our progress kept?

On the iPhone itself. Nothing is sent anywhere, so deleting the app deletes the game. Removing a player in the game deletes that player's game.

Where did the story go?

It's all still there, just out of the way of riding. Open the Kingdom Book and tap Read the story, or turn the story cards back on in Settings > Story.

Can the story be read out loud?

Yes: Settings > Read the story aloud. It uses the iPhone's built-in voice, and it stays quiet while VoiceOver is on.

Why did a land change?

Some lands follow the calendar: the season's land, and holiday lands such as Spooky Hollow in October or Christmas Village in December. A grown-up can preview any month in Settings > Parents' corner (a grown-up question keeps kids out). It goes back to Today the next time the game is opened.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap the + button, choose Sparklecorn, and pick the small or medium size. It shows your beast and which land is in the kingdom today.

Are there ads or purchases?

No. No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no tracking. Gems are only ever earned by playing.
